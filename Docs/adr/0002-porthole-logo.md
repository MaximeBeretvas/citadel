# Citadel's logo is the Porthole mark

Citadel's identity is the **Porthole**: a lens framing a walled skyline under a rising beacon. The lens stands for search and query, the wall for the local, fortified store, and the beacon for docs kept fresh. The ring, wall and wordmark are Citadel Navy `#0F172A`. The beacon is Beacon Blue `#2563EB` on light backgrounds and Beacon Light `#60A5FA` on dark ones. A separate small-size cut (thicker ring, two battlements, bigger beacon) is used at 32 px and below. The wordmark is a custom geometric lowercase `citadel` built from the same rings, as outlines rather than a font. All master files, variants, web icons, the presentation board and the usage rules live in `Docs/assets/logo/`. The masters are generated by `Docs/assets/logo/build.py`, and `Docs/assets/logo/GUIDELINES.md` holds the usage rules.

## Considered Options

- **v1 set (Google Neon), 10 icons:** five Citadel and five Holocron marks. The blue *High Tower Beacon* was picked first and then superseded. It was too detailed: its flame and windows blur at 16 px, and it relied on a glow.
- **v2 set (Claude Opus 5.5), 10 icons:** simpler isometric and fortress tiles. These were exploration only and never chosen.
- **v3 concepts, the other nine:** Rampart C, Beacon Tower, Bracket Keep, Page Keep, Star Fort, Gate Cursor, Isometric Keep, Concentric Walls and Bookmark Keep. They were rejected on a mix of second readings (Star Fort read as a throwing star, Beacon Tower as a lighthouse), legibility at 16 px, and fit. Gate Cursor was recommended, but the Porthole was chosen.
- **Name "Holocron":** dropped in favour of Citadel (see the rename commit `016583b`).

All candidates remain in git history at commit `3ab6e44` and were removed from the tree.

## Consequences

- The Citadel web UI still uses its green accent (`--accent: #15803d` in `src/ui/assets/style.css`) and a generic header icon. Two follow-ups remain: wire in `Docs/assets/logo/web/` (favicon set and head snippet) and the symbol as the header icon, and decide whether the UI accent moves to Beacon Blue. Until then, don't mix the blue and the green in the same component.
- `favicon.svg` adapts to dark mode. The PNG and ICO favicons sit on a navy tile so they read on any tab colour.
- The Pantone and CMYK values in the guidelines are approximate. The mark has not been trademark-cleared; run a professional search before any public launch.
